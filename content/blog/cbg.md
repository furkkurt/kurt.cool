## What exactly is a CBG?

It's basicly a handmade guitar using a stick as the neck and a cigar box as the body and that's where the name comes from. You have complete freedom about the strings; it can have 4 strings like a uke, 6 strings like a guitar or 5 strings like a banjo or something completly new and different but the most common setup is going with 3 strings giving you an open power chord like GDG or EBE. If you go with the open power chord build you usually play it with a slide. 

### What do you need to make your own build?

Just a long stick, a box, strings, frets and a cheville. Frets and cheville(s) can be found easily in guitar stores. And as for tools you don't really need power tools; just a saw, a file, a hammer and some nails should do. Also some linseed oild will help a lot.
